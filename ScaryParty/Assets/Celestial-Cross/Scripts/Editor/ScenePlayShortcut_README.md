# Scene Play Shortcut (Ctrl+G) for Unity

A lightweight editor utility that drastically improves your workflow by allowing you to always enter Play Mode in a specific "Main" scene, regardless of which scene you are currently editing.

## Features
- **Global Play Shortcut (Ctrl+G)**: Instantly play the game from your pre-configured entry scene.
- **Auto-Save**: Prompts you to save your current open scenes before transitioning.
- **Easy Configuration**: Simple popup window to drag and drop your main scene.

## How to use
1. In the Unity Editor, go to `Tools > Configurações do Atalho de Play (Ctrl+G)`.
2. A small window will appear. Drag and drop your Main Menu / Boot scene into the slot.
3. While working on ANY scene in your project, press **Ctrl+G** on your keyboard.
4. The editor will save your current scene, load your Boot scene, and hit Play!
